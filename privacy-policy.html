Overview

Monarch Liquor ("we," "us") operates monarchliquor.com. This site is an informational website for our two Austin-area stores. We keep things simple: we don't run our own accounts, logins, or payment systems on this site, and we don't sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

This website itself does not collect personal information through forms or accounts. When you contact us by phone or email, we use the information you provide only to respond to your inquiry.

Online ordering is handled by our e-commerce partner, BottleCapps, at monarchliquor.bottlecapps.com. When you place an order there, the information you provide (name, address, payment details, date of birth) is collected and processed under BottleCapps' own privacy practices. Please review their policies on their site before ordering.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing the data it processes.
